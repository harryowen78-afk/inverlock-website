Our Approach

Independent commercial capability for energy and infrastructure.

Organisations are built to build. Ambition, momentum, and institutional bias all push in one direction. Inverlock provides the independent commercial capability to test whether that direction is right, and to act when it isn't.

01

Investment Assurance

Major capital commitment approaching. The board needs independent challenge before committing.

What we do

Independently test economics, risk allocation, and delivery readiness. Challenge assumptions that internal teams have lived with too long to question. Surface single points of failure that financial sensitivity analysis won't catch.

How we work

Retained pre-FID to stress-test the investment case under real delivery conditions. We operate alongside management, not above them.

Capital committed with conviction. Decision backed by independent view.

02

Growth & Capital Discipline

Scaling into new markets, assets, or geographies. Growth ambitions need grounding against capital reality.

What we do

Test growth plans against funding headroom, balance sheet capacity, and delivery capability. Separate viable pipeline from expensive optionality. Remove speculative options that delay proper decisions.

How we work

We help with growth planning precisely because we know what happens when growth goes wrong. That perspective is the value.

Growth scaled at a pace the returns can support, not ambition alone.

03

Performance & Strategic Review

A business may no longer justify the capital it absorbs. Cost base, targets, and organisation are misaligned to market reality.

What we do

Whole-business review of performance, capital efficiency, and strategic options. From continued investment through restructuring to exit. Every option is sized by cost, value impact, and execution risk.

How we work

We produce a clear recommendation, not a menu of options without a view. The board gets a defined path forward with a position behind it.

A decision the board can stand behind.

04

Exit & Recovery

A project, portfolio, or business unit requires a controlled wind-down. Contractual exposure is high, stakeholder complexity is real.

What we do

Build a decision-grade view of contractual exposure. Execute termination programmes, settle contracts, monetise or repurpose assets. We have run these programmes at scale and owned the outcomes.

How we work

Advisory first, then execution. We scope the problem, define solutions, and stay to deliver them. That second phase is where the value compounds.

Clean exit executed, capital released, obligations discharged.
