Our Approach

Organisations are built to build. Ambition, momentum, and institutional bias all push in one direction. Inverlock provides the independent commercial capability to test whether that direction is right, and to act when it isn't.

01

Investment Assurance

Major capital commitment approaching. The board needs independent challenge before committing.

What we do

Independently test economics, risk allocation, and delivery readiness. Challenge assumptions that internal teams have lived with too long to question. Surface single points of failure that financial models won't catch.

Outcome

Capital committed with conviction. Investment case stress-tested, risks surfaced, decision backed by independent view.

02

Growth & Capital Discipline

Scaling into new markets, assets, or geographies. Growth ambitions need grounding against capital constraints.

What we do

Test growth plans against funding headroom, returns, and delivery capability. Separate viable pipeline from expensive optionality. Remove speculative options that delay real decisions.

Outcome

Growth scaled at a pace the economics support, not ambition alone.

03

Performance & Strategic Review

A business or portfolio may no longer justify the capital it absorbs. Cost base, targets, and organisation are misaligned.

What we do

Whole-business review of performance, capital efficiency, and strategic options. From continued investment through restructuring to exit. A clear recommendation the board can act on.

Outcome

A decision the board can stand behind, with a defined path forward.

04

Exit & Recovery

A project, portfolio, or business unit requires a controlled wind-down. Contractual exposure is high, stakeholder complexity is real.

What we do

Build a decision-grade view of exposure. Execute termination programmes, settle contracts, monetise or repurpose assets. We have run these programmes at scale and owned the outcomes.

Outcome

Clean exit executed, capital released, obligations discharged.
